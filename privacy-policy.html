Privacy Policy — Brainrot: Tactical Aura Rush

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what data Brainrot: Tactical Aura Rush ("the App") collects and how it is used. The App is developed by Michael Scofield, contact: mscofield1588@gmail.com.

Data the App itself collects

The App does not require an account, login, or personal information to play, and the App itself does not transmit any personal data anywhere.

The App saves a small amount of gameplay data on your device only: your best aura score, the furthest zone you have reached, and whether sound is muted. This data never leaves your device.

Advertising

The App does not display ads.

The App displays ads served by Google AdMob (a Google service) to support free play. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect data such as your advertising ID, device information, general (IP-based) location, and ad interaction data, and may use this data to personalize ads unless you have opted out of ad personalization in your device settings. This collection and use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's EU User Consent Policy.
